## STOP · 17 words

Your board will ask what your position on AI is. Not what the company is doing. Yours.

## BELIEF · 56 words

And you would feel reasonably covered. You have sat through the briefings. Someone forwards you a piece most weeks and you read the first half. There is a person on your team who owns AI and reports on it. When it comes up you can hold your end of the conversation. That has always been enough.

## TURN · 113 words

Except the question changed direction, and nobody announced it.

It used to be asked downward. What are we doing about AI. You could hand that to a deputy and the room accepted it. That was delegation, and it looked like leadership.

Now it gets asked upward. What is your position. Your board asks in the language of risk and money. Your team asks it silently, every time a decision sits on your desk because nobody is sure what you think.

And here is the difference that matters. An opinion is what you picked up. A position is what you built. From the inside they feel identical. From the outside they look nothing alike.

## COST · 94 words

And the outside is where it gets tested.

An opinion survives the first question and falls apart on the second, because it was never yours to defend. Your board has sat through enough borrowed conviction to spot it in one exchange.

And handing the question down does not read as delegation any more. It reads as absence.

Meanwhile somebody in your world is becoming the voice people trust on this. Your board will find one. Your team will find one. Usually someone with less responsibility than you and more confidence than they have earned.

## MOVE · 65 words

So, two hours on your own calendar this week. Alone, with one question. If my board asked me tomorrow to state our AI position in five sentences, what would I say?

Write the five. Do not hand the draft to anyone, because the point is not the paper. It is finding out how much of your answer is borrowed. The briefing is in my bio.

## CLOSE · 27 words

The confusion you have been quietly carrying is not a personal failing. It is what happens when you get briefed by everyone and equipped by no one.
