## STOP · 15 words

Your best new revenue line this year is something you already turned down. Twice, probably.

## BELIEF · 58 words

And when AI comes up as a money question, you think efficiency. Same work, fewer hours, better margin. Maybe someone has already shown you a tool that drafts your reports. It is real, you have probably banked some of it, and it is the smallest version of what actually happened. Nobody has told you to look anywhere else.

## TURN · 110 words

Because AI did one thing that actually matters to your margins. It collapsed the cost of the preparation.

Think about any premium service you sell. Most of the hours in it are not the expert judgment people pay for. They are the drafting, the structuring, the summarizing, the first pass at the analysis. The judgment sits in the last twenty percent.

That judgment is still yours. Still scarce. Still the thing they are buying. What got cheap is everything wrapped around it.

So the opportunity is not selling what you sell now for less. It is that services which never made money at last year's costs now clear the bar.

## COST · 90 words

And that is the part worth sitting with.

Every request you have politely declined on cost is market research somebody handed you for free. If three clients asked for the same thing and you said no three times, somebody is going to build it. The only open question is whether it carries your judgment or a competitor's.

And they are looking at the same arithmetic you are. Most of them have not re-read their own list either. That gap is the whole advantage, and it is not a long one.

## MOVE · 68 words

So, one hour this week. Write down what customers keep asking for that you keep saying no to on cost. Then re-cost the strongest one with the preparation handled.

And do not price it as a discount. They are not buying your lower costs, they are buying the thing they could not get before. The other two places to look are in the briefing, linked in my bio.

## CLOSE · 38 words

You have been keeping that list of noes in your head for years. Somewhere on it, a no became a yes while nobody was looking, and it is worth real money to be the first one who notices.

- **The link is named once, in MOVE.** The close lands with no sign-off.

- TURN has three fragments in a row: "Still scarce. Still the thing they are buying." They are the rhythmic centre of the script. Do not smooth them into one sentence.
