After the lock, Home, the Roadmap, Next Steps and the Profile stay open. **Only Day 3 reopens in AI Labs** (founder, 2026-10-10): it holds the Milestone Task, and many will come back to it. - **Day 7 takes a selection, not everything.** Only the most important or appropriate material moves.
- **The menu is left for later.**
- **The AISC site copy is the last thing done,** on the founder's word.

1. **The Roadmap, written on Day 6, asks for the mandate.** Section 1 is "My Personal Mandate" and tells the leader to draw on the three dimensions "you encountered in this programme". If the Mandate is first taught on Day 7, the Roadmap asks for it a day early.
2. **The Personal AI Policy is a prerequisite of the Roadmap.** It feeds the Executive Summary, so today it must be written before Day 6 is finished.
3. **Day 3 opens on the Mandate.** The session is "Mandate, Money, Map", and the ethics self-assessment is placed "before you move into the territory and value work of today". Without Part 1, Day 3 is "Money, Map", which matches its theme word, Money.
4. **Day 3's wrap-up and title slides** name the Mandate.
5. **The Day 3 page and the Toolbox** link the Mandate & Ethics Guide as a Day 3 resource.
6. **The AI Safety Guidelines were moved to Labs whole** (founder, 2026-10-08: "Don't split it. Move it."). They are governance material by subject. Whether "all governance material" includes them is a question for the founder.
7. **The new Day 2 already touches governance twice** (2.7 and 2.8). Those were written as readiness, about the leader personally, and can stay as the first sight of something Day 7 finishes.
