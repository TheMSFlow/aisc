## Slide 14: the Curator becomes the Convener

| | Now | New |
|---|---|---|
| Label | POSITION 5 | POSITION 5 |
| Name | Curator | Convener |
| Tagline | Turn knowledge into a trusted asset | Bring people together around what no one can solve alone |
| Best for | Leaders with high research discipline… | Leaders others will answer an invitation from: heads of industry bodies, associations, communities, congregations and constituencies. Your advantage is trust across groups that do not otherwise work together, and the standing to rally people around an idea, even when you do not have the time to build it yourself. |
| What this position does | The Curator researches, evaluates… | The Convener builds relationships and community around one friction that no single organisation can fix. They bring the right people to the table, keep them there, and turn a shared concern into shared action. They do not need to own the solution. They make it possible for others to build it, adopt it, and carry it further together. |
| Examples | Producing trusted assessments… · Curating intelligence… · Building a publication… | Bringing rival firms in an industry together on a problem none of them can fix alone, such as fraud · Running a joint AI readiness programme for the members of an association or network · Bringing employers, schools and officials together around a workforce transition · Gathering the faith leaders of a city to agree on a shared response to AI |
| Question | What AI knowledge, if I made it reliably available, would people in my domain pay for or seek out repeatedly? | What friction in my world will only move if the right people move together, and would they come to the table for me? |
