# AI Readiness Check policy

**Michael Steve Clarity Studio** ("we") runs aistakeholderchallenge.com and the AI Stakeholder Challenge. This policy explains what we collect when you take our free AI readiness check, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

When you take the free AI readiness check, we collect:

- **Your first name and email address.**
- **Your answers and your scores**: how you rated the 24 statements, your six scores, your strongest dimension and where you chose to start.
- **What you asked for**: whether you asked to join our private WhatsApp community, whether you asked us to email you later, the exact words of each box you ticked, and when.
- **Where you came from**: the link or social post that brought you, the website that sent you, and the country your connection is in.
- **A scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address**, used only to stop the form being misused.

We do **not** keep the notes you write beside a statement. They go into your own report and nowhere else.

## Why we collect it

- **To send you your report.** Your report is built from your answers and sent to your email.
- **To send you the invite to our private WhatsApp community**, if you ticked the box that asks for it.
- **To send you practical notes on AI and news of our programs**, only if you ticked the box that asks for them. You can stop them at any time.
- **To improve the check and our programs**, by looking at results across everyone who takes it.

If you leave the email box unticked, we use your email for your report and nothing else. Your report is the same either way.

## The private WhatsApp community

If you ask to join, we show you the invite on the page and include it in your report email. Joining is your choice, and you join through WhatsApp yourself. We do not add your number.

WhatsApp, a service run by Meta, runs the community under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Who else handles your data

We use a small number of trusted services to run the check, and they handle your data only on our behalf:

- **Resend**, which sends the email with your report.
- **Supabase**, which stores your answers and your choices.
- **Vercel**, which hosts our websites.

These services may store data outside your country, including in the United States. *[to confirm, optional: the regions used, if you want them named]*

**We do not sell your data**, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

## Your choices

- **Ask to see, correct or delete your data**, by sending us a message (a link to the inquiry form, `intelligence.michaelsteve.com/form/inquiry?src=AISC`, because site pages show no raw email address). We will reply within 30 days. *[to confirm: 30 days is the proposal on the page]*
- **Stop our notes and news** at any time. Every one we send carries a way to stop them.
- **Leave the WhatsApp community** at any time, from within WhatsApp.
- If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you may complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Nigeria, that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date below. The wording of each box you ticked is kept with your record, so a later change never alters what you asked for.

## Review notes, not for publication

- **The checkboxes are optional** (founder, 2026-10-24), so everyone gets their report whether or not they tick them, and no consent is bundled with the free check.
- **"Nothing else"** is a promise to those who leave the email box unticked. They cannot be emailed later on the strength of this check.
- **The email box** (founder, 2026-10-24, with the `leads` table) records permission only. Nothing is sent on it until there is a way to stop the emails, which "Every one we send carries a way to stop them" promises (`WORKING_RULES.md` rule 29).
- **Phone numbers:** the founder's group shows members' numbers to each other (2026-10-25). The policy does not mention numbers, at his word: "Saying it's a private whatsapp group is good enough." It must never say that numbers are hidden. The success page and the report email say each request to join is approved by hand, which he signed off with the email on 2026-10-24.
- **No retention period is stated** (founder, 2026-10-25: "Remove the 'How long we keep it' part").
- **The contact route** is the inquiry form rather than an address, because the marketing sites show no raw email addresses (SOURCE_OF_TRUTH, email routing map). Requests arrive in the admin inbox under `AISC`.
- **The regulator** named is Nigeria's, since most of the audience is in Nigeria. "Where you live" covers everyone else.
