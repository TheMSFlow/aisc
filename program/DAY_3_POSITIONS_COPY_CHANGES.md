**Recommended: rewrite it as "Six Positions a Leader Can Take on AI".** The argument stays the same (a position beats a strategy, choose one, three questions to find yours). What changes:

- **Title, description and slug:** "Six Positions a Leader Can Take on AI", "…Convener, Guardian. The six stakeholder positions on AI…", slug `six-positions-a-leader-can-take-on-ai`, with the topic ledger's rows updated to match.

- **The Curator's paragraph goes, and two are added:**
  - **The Convener** brings people together around a friction no single organization can fix. Some of the most expensive problems in any industry sit between organizations, where no one owns them: fraud that moves between banks, a skills gap that spans employers and schools. The Convener does not need to build the solution. Their win is the table itself: the right people in the room, staying there, and acting together. It fits leaders whose invitation people answer, even when they have no time to build anything themselves.
  - **The Guardian** sets the standards that let AI be trusted. Every institution is now deciding, often by default, what AI may be used for, what must be disclosed, and what people are owed. The Guardian makes those decisions on purpose, for a whole agency, company, school system or denomination. Good standards do not slow AI down; they are what lets it spread safely. It fits leaders whose authority already shapes how others operate.
- **For creators** (the briefing is tagged for them), one new paragraph after the six: "A job title or a tool is not a position. Creating content with AI, video included, is a powerful means, but not a position. Content that teaches makes you an Educator. Content that guides decisions makes you an Advisor. Content people use or pay for makes you a Producer. Content that gathers people around a cause makes you a Convener."
- **The last paragraph:** "the Territory Builder assigns your Competitive Position on Day 3" becomes "you choose your stakeholder position on Day 3, in the AI Value Map and the Territory Builder".
