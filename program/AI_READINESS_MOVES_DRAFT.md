# AI readiness check: the 30 moves (approved)

**Status:** approved by the founder on 19 November 2026 ("Approve, build it") and built: `intelligence/src/lib/readiness/scorecard.js` holds each move (`move` on a statement, `strengthMove` on a dimension) and `movesFor()` chooses them; the AISC page applies the same rule (`aisc/src/lib/readiness/profile.js`). Change a move there, and here, together.

**For:** the free public AI readiness check only (results page, report email and PDF). Day 2 in the Learning Center keeps its own six moves.

## How a move is chosen

You decided: *"In every dimension, we start with the lowest score in that dimension and give a recommendation for it."*

1. **Every dimension shows the move for its lowest-rated statement.** If two or more statements share the lowest rating, the first in the list is used.
2. **A strength (14 to 20) also shows its strength move**, under the statement move, so the leader builds on what is working.
3. **When all four answers are 4 or 5, only the strength move shows.** A 4 means "Yes", so a move to fix that statement would tell them to do something they already do. This can only happen in a strength.

So a leader sees one move in each gap and each developing dimension, and one or two in each strength. Every move is small enough to do this week.

**Your example:** a Data Governance of 15 where "I have marked which of my information is personal or confidential" is rated 2 shows the move for that statement (D4) and the strength move. It never mentions duplicate copies.

## Strategy

*Do I have a plan for AI, tied to where I am going?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | I can name the problems in my work that AI could help with, and say which matters most. | Write down three problems in your work that AI could help with. Beside each, note what it costs you now in time or money. Circle the one that matters most. |
| S2 | I have written down why I want to use AI and how it links to my goals. | Write two sentences: why you want to use AI, and which of your goals it would help you reach. |
| S3 | In the last 3 months, I set aside time to decide where AI fits in my priorities. | Put 30 minutes in your diary this week to decide where AI fits in your priorities. Leave with one priority it could help and one it should not touch. |
| S4 | I can explain in one sentence why I want to use AI. | Finish this sentence in under 20 words: "I want to use AI so that…". Say it to one person this week and ask if it makes sense to them. |
| **Strength** | | Share your plan for AI with one person you work with, and ask where it could help their work too. |

## Impact Evaluation

*Do I measure results, risks and consequences?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Before I change how I do a task, I write down what a good result would look like. | Pick one task you want to change. Before you change anything, write down what a good result would look like. |
| I2 | I know how much time, money or mistakes my main tasks cost me today, so I would notice if a change helped. | Pick your main task and write down what it costs you today: hours each week, money, or the mistakes it causes. |
| I3 | When I look at a new way of working, I think about what could go wrong, not only what I gain. | Take one change you are thinking about. Write down three things that could go wrong, beside what you would gain. |
| I4 | I check my results on a date I set in advance, not only when something goes wrong. | Pick one change you have made, and put a date in your diary to check whether it worked. |
| **Strength** | | Show one colleague how you measure results, and help them set one measure for a task of their own. |

## Data Governance

*Is my data within reach, of good quality and well managed?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | I can list the information my decisions depend on (for example, client lists, budgets, sales figures) and where each one is kept. | List the five pieces of information your decisions depend on most, such as client lists, budgets or sales figures. Beside each, write where it is kept. |
| D2 | For each one, I know who is responsible for it. | Beside each piece of information you rely on, write the name of the person responsible for keeping it right. Where there is no name, that is your gap. |
| D3 | For my most important records, I know which copy is the right one to use. | Pick the record you rely on most. Write down every place a copy of it is kept, and decide which one is the right one to use. |
| D4 | I have marked which of my information is personal or confidential, so I know what I am allowed to share. | Go through the information you rely on and mark each piece as open, personal or confidential. Use it to decide what you may share, including with an AI tool. |
| **Strength** | | Write down how you keep your best-managed record in order, so the people you work with can do the same. |

## Talent

*Do I have the understanding of AI and the knowledge of my work that I need, and do I know what I lack?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | I can explain in simple words what AI can and cannot do in my kind of work. | Write two short lists: what AI can do in your kind of work, and what it cannot. Ask one colleague whether they agree. |
| T2 | In the last 6 months, I have had some training on how AI can help my work. | Choose one short training on AI for work like yours, and put the date in your diary this week. |
| T3 | I know which AI skills I am missing, and for each one, whether I will learn it, hire someone or work with a partner. | List the AI skills you are missing. Beside each, write whether you will learn it, hire for it or work with a partner. |
| T4 | I know my work well enough to say where AI would help and where it would not. | Take one part of your work you know well. Write down where AI could help with it, where it should not, and why. |
| **Strength** | | Explain to one person in your team what AI can and cannot do in your work. Teaching it will show you what you still need to learn. |

## Processes

*Is AI part of how my work gets done, or still an experiment on the side?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | The main tasks I do again and again are written down step by step, not just kept in my head. | Take one task you do every week and write its steps in order, as if someone else had to do it tomorrow. |
| P2 | For those tasks, I know which steps AI could do, which it could help with, and which I must do myself. | Take one task you have written down. Beside each step, mark whether AI could do it, could help with it, or should leave it to you. |
| P3 | When I use AI for a task I do often, I use it the same way each time, not differently every time. (If you do not use AI yet, choose 1.) | Pick one task you do often. Write down, step by step, how AI should help with it, so it is done the same way every time. If you do not use AI yet, write this before you start. |
| P4 | When I try a new way of working, I decide at the end whether to stop it, change it or keep it. | Think of one new way of working you tried recently. Decide today whether to stop it, change it or keep it, and write down why. |
| **Strength** | | Share one task you have written down step by step with a colleague, so they can use it too. |

## Collaboration

*Do I work on AI with others, or alone?*

| | Statement | Move |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | When I think about AI, I ask for ideas from someone whose work is different from mine. | Ask one person whose work is different from yours where AI could help in your work. Write down what they see that you did not. |
| C2 | I talk early to the people in charge of the rules I must follow (such as legal or HR), not only at the end. | Find out who is in charge of the rules you must follow, such as legal or HR. Tell them early about one way you plan to use AI, and ask what to watch for. |
| C3 | I can admit openly when something I tried did not work. | Write down one thing you tried recently that did not work, and what it taught you. Share it with one person. |
| C4 | In the last 3 months, I told someone what I learned about AI, including what did not work. | Tell one person this week what you have learned about AI so far, including one thing that did not work. |
| **Strength** | | Bring one colleague into your next conversation about AI, so the way you work with others spreads. |

## What changes once you approve

- **Results page:** each accordion shows its move (or two in a strength), labelled "Your next move" and, for a strength, "Build on your strength".
- **Report email:** "Your next move, for [dimension number 1]" uses the move chosen for that dimension.
- **PDF:** each section shows the same move or moves as the page.
- **Stays the same:** the statements, questions, scale and scores, and Day 2.
