# Find your path policy

**Michael Steve Clarity Studio** ("we") runs aistakeholderchallenge.com and the AI Stakeholder Challenge. This policy explains what we collect when you use Find your path, the questions that recommend a way into the challenge, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

**When you finish the questions**, whether or not you ask for the email, we keep:

- **Your answers**: your seat, your currency, and what you typed in answer to the questions.
- **The recommendation** you were shown.
- **A scrambled (hashed) form of your internet address and the kind of browser you used**, used only to stop the page being misused.

**If you ask us to email you the recommendation**, we also keep:

- **Your first name and email address.**
- **Whether you asked us to email you later**, the exact words of the box you ticked, and when.
- **Where you came from**: the link or social post that brought you, the website that sent you, and the country your connection is in.

## Why we collect it

- **To write your recommendation.** Your answers are used to choose and explain the way into the challenge that fits you.
- **To send you your recommendation**, if you ask for it.
- **To send you practical notes on AI and news of our programs**, only if you ticked the box that asks for them. You can stop them at any time.
- **To improve Find your path and our programs**, by looking at answers across everyone who uses it.

If you leave the email box unticked, we use your email to send your recommendation and nothing else.

## Who else handles your data

We use a small number of trusted services, and they handle your data only on our behalf:

- **Anthropic**, whose AI (Claude) writes your recommendation from your answers.
- **Resend**, which sends the email with your recommendation.
- **Supabase**, which stores your answers and your choices.
- **Vercel**, which hosts our websites.

These services may store data outside your country, including in the United States.

**We do not sell your data**, and we do not share it with anyone else for their own purposes.

## Your choices

- **Ask to see, correct or delete your data**, by sending us a message (a link to the inquiry form, `intelligence.michaelsteve.com/form/inquiry?src=AISC`). We will reply within 30 days.
- **Stop our notes and news** at any time. Every one we send carries a way to stop them.
- If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you may complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Nigeria, that is the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date below. The wording of the box you ticked is kept with your record, so a later change never alters what you asked for.

*Last updated: 25 October 2026*
