## 2. The line that keeps the quality

The blog teaches the **question** each dimension asks and **why it matters**. The **measuring and the moves** stay in the check and in Day 2. So no briefing:

- reproduces the 24 statements, the full set of first moves, the worksheets or the Data Readiness Audit's steps. These belong to participants (the documents say so), and they are what the check and the challenge give a reader;
- names the outside course or assessment your notes and the template came from, or the maturity model your notes name. Their insights appear as AISC's own choices, or as what "most readiness tests" do (`WORKING_RULES.md` rule 24);
- promises hands-on work with AI, hours recovered or a pilot inside the seven days. Those happen in Labs.

Each briefing may give **one** small move of its own, so it is worth reading on its own, and then point to the check where that is the natural next step.
